Problem & Community Need

🌱 Problem Identified

  • Trees are planted without checking if the location and soil are suitable
  • Planted trees are often not properly monitored or watered
  • Some trees die after planting due to lack of maintenance

👥 Who Faces This Problem?

  • Students and colleges
  • Local communities
  • Municipal authorities
  • NGOs and environmental groups

📍 Where / When Does It Occur?

  • Roadsides
  • School and college campuses
  • Empty and open areas
  • Urban areas with less greenery
  • During and after plantation drives

💡 Why Does It Need a Solution?

  • Focus should be on tree survival, not just trees planted
  • Proper species selection and regular monitoring can improve survival
  • More healthy trees means better air quality, shade and biodiversity
"A plantation drive may plant 100 trees, but without proper watering and monitoring, many young trees may not survive."

How SmartTree AI Works

📋 Data / Input Required

  • 📍 Location
  • 🟤 Soil type
  • ☁️ Weather information
  • 🌿 Tree species
  • 📷 Tree photograph
  • 📅 Planting date

✅ Expected Output

  • 🌳 Suitable tree recommendation
  • 💧 Watering requirement
  • ❤️ Tree health status
  • ⚠️ Warning if tree appears unhealthy
  • 📊 Tree growth record

⚙️ Workflow

1
User Input
User enters location, soil type, tree species and photos
2
Location + Soil + Tree Image / Data
All environmental context is collected for analysis
3
AI Model
Machine learning model processes the combined inputs
4
Data Processing & Analysis
Soil suitability, weather correlation, species matching
5
Tree Recommendation + Health Prediction
Best species selected; health forecast generated
6
Watering / Maintenance Reminder
Automated schedule sent to user
7
User
User acts on recommendations and logs progress
